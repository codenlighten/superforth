    Every artifact (source → IR → WAT/WASM → Verilog → manifest) is signed with hybrid PQ + ECDSA and anchored to an immutable ledger. Independent verifiers can cryptographically prove authenticity, timestamp, authorship, and full derivation chain—without trusting us.

    Hybrid Signatures

    ML‑DSA‑87 (post-quantum) + ECDSA (traditional) on every artifact. Future-proof against cryptanalytic breaks and quantum threats.

    Assessors rebuild artifacts offline and compare hashes to chain anchors. Validates reproducibility without trusting the builder.
